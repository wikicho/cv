# Wonsub Cho — Curriculum Vitae

This repository contains the LaTeX source for my academic curriculum vitae.

## Research profile

I am a Ph.D. candidate in theoretical particle physics at Sungkyunkwan University. My research focuses on dark-matter phenomenology and early-Universe cosmology, including dark radiation, relic-abundance calculations, sterile neutrinos, first-order phase transitions, and baryogenesis.

## Highlights

- Five first-author peer-reviewed publications in *Physics of the Dark Universe*, *Journal of Cosmology and Astroparticle Physics*, and *Physical Review D*.
- Research on inelastic dark matter and cosmological tensions, Pauli-blocking stabilization of dark matter, dark-matter--neutrino interactions, sterile-neutrino dark matter, and boosted dark matter.
- Oral and poster presentations at international and Korean physics meetings.
- Teaching-assistant experience in quantum field theory, electromagnetism, relativity, mathematical physics, and general physics.
